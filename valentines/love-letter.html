Dear Armel

I’ve been thinking a lot about everything, and I want to start by saying I’m sorry. I know I’ve never been the boyfriend you truly deserve, and I haven’t always tried as hard as I should have. Looking back, I can see how I fell short. At the time, it felt like I was doing my best, but I now realize I could have done so much more for you.

You mean more to me than anything in this world. You’ve been my strength, my support, and the reason I’ve made it through some of my darkest moments. When things seemed impossible, when I felt like I couldn’t go on anymore, you were there. You pulled me out of the deep moments of sadness, made me feel cared for when I felt completely alone, and helped me see light when everything felt dark. Your smile has gotten me through so many tough days, and for that, I will always be grateful.

I’ve been through so much in my past—pain that sometimes felt like it would swallow me whole. From being in and out of hospitals as a child, feeling lost and misunderstood, to the moments when I didn’t think I could make it through another day. And yet, through all of that, I met you. You became the one thing that made me believe in something better, in something real. Your love, your kindness, and the way you’re always there for me—it has helped me heal in ways I never thought possible.

I’m truly sorry for the things I’ve said and done in the past. I know my promises may feel empty right now, given how many I’ve broken. But I want you to know that I’m trying harder than I ever have before. I’m committed to doing better, not just for me, but for us, because I love you more than I can express. I want to be the partner you deserve—the person who supports you the way you support me.

Thank you for being the light in my life. For giving me love when I felt like I didn’t deserve it. For standing by me even when I didn’t know how to be the best for you. You are priceless to me, more than I could ever show or say. I am so lucky to have you.

I love you, more than anything. Tilen
